## Program Overview

eRUPT (eBay's Research and University Partnership for Technology) offers 1-year grants for academic research collaboration projects. Grants can be structured as:
- Sponsored research (gift funding), or
- No-fee research projects

## Submission Requirements

### Format
Proposals must answer the following questions (in order):

1. **Objectives**
   - What are you trying to do? What is the problem?
   - Why is it useful / interesting?
   - Why is it a good candidate for academic research rather than doing it in-house?

2. **Previous Work**
   - Describe any relevant previous project and/or publication.
   - If this is a continuation, show previous results (business impact if applicable).

3. **Methodologies / Approaches**
   - How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?
   - What is new in your approach and why do you think it will be successful?

4. **Impact**
   - How does it relate to the problems eBay is trying to solve?
   - If successful, what difference will it make?
   - What applications are enabled as a result?

5. **Expected Product / Output / Milestones**
   - What are the risks?
   - What are the midterm and final checks / metrics for success?
   - PoC models and algorithms, reports, publications, experiments, learnings…

6. **University / Lab / Researcher Qualification**
   - Who will be the university researchers eBay will engage with?
   - Description of their work, links, etc.

7. **References**

### Template
- Custom eBay template (`.docx` format provided: `files/eRUPT 2026_27 Template for Academic Research Collaboration Proposals.docx`)
- Page limit: Not explicitly stated (template is ~2-3 pages of content)

### Submission Process
- Work with eBay Co-Investigator to submit
- Email submissions to: **erupt@ebay.com**

## Intellectual Property Terms

- eBay expects, at minimum, royalty-free license rights to resulting project IP without additional licensing costs.
- Open sourcing results under a permissive license may be an option depending on the nature of the research.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Relevance to eBay | Proposals must be relevant to eBay's problems |
| State of the Art | Potential to advance the state of the art |
| Practical Application | eBay is interested in practical application of results |

All proposals will be considered provided they meet the above criteria.
